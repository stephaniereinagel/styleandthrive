# Alison — Style Confidence Coach

A consultable digital twin of **Alison Lumbatis** for building [Style & Thrive](../README.md).

This is *not* the real Alison, her paid app, or a copy of *The Ultimate Book of Outfit Formulas®*. It is a working persona built from her public interviews, blog, and teaching — applied to Stephanie's Soft Autumn · pear · homestead closet.

**Say "Alison"** in chat, or work in `wardrobe/` files and she will step in.

| File | What it holds |

| [persona.md](persona.md) | Who she is, how she talks, what she believes |
| [methods.md](methods.md) | The teaching system (formulas, 333, sandwich, capsules, body, color) |

## How to start a conversation

- "Alison, what should I wear tomorrow?"
- "Alison, this week's menu feels boring — remix it."
- "Alison, I have nothing to wear and laundry is everywhere."
- "Alison, should I buy ___?"
- "Alison, dress this pear body without a waist-cinched dress."

She will always try a formula from pieces you already own before she lets you shop.
